# Econews
Game design and development for climate change news reading and annotation

By Dorina Rajanen

The Econews game design and development is part of a research project at University of Oulu. The project was initiated 
by Dorina Rajanen (principal investigator) starting in November 2025. Current team members are 
Riikka Tumelius (co-PI) and Mikko Rajanen.

The project designs and pilots a serious game to study human-AI collaboration in interpreting climate news. 
The project aims to collect data on user engagement with climate information and 
interaction with an AI agent that categorizes news and negotiates meaning with players. Players 
compare their categorizations with the AI and engage in dialogue when differences occur, contrib-
uting to research on climate communication and human-AI collaboration. We examine how users 
interact with the news to understand communication strategies and framing [1], the individual en-
gagement [2,3], and the nature of the human-AI dialogue as well as its outcome or result. 

The game concept and the project uses a citizen science approach [4], allowing players to contribute 
real-world data through gameplay. The AI agent supports both game mechanics and the learning process, 
enhancing cognitive, emotional, behavioral, and agentic engagement [2,5]. By categorizing news articles, 
players actively participate in climate communication research. 

Tasks in game design and game development with student group (to update):
- Brainstorming and ideation (all team/1-2 weeks)
- News annotations (1-2 students/2 weeks)
- Design and implementation of the game (front-end and back-end design and development) (group/1-2 months);
- Playtests: Gameplay sessions with users in controlled environment, data collection of gameplay and AI 
interactions (group/2 weeks);
- Data collection of news categories generated in the game (database) (1-2 students/1-2 weeks)

Deliverables:
- Conceptual and digital, functional game prototype;
- Functional AI agent as game character;
- Functional database;
- Research datasets on human-AI interactions,
- Gameplay recordings and analyses

## Current development status

### Version 1
In Spring 2026, a first version of the game prototype was developed by a student team as part of their R&D project course at University of Oulu.
The contributors include: Aleksi Kantola, Md Shafiul Alam, Jooa Tammi, Sampsa Jalli, Karoliina Autio, and Shadman Riffat.
The developement used Unity as the programming environment and Figma as the design environment.
A WebGL demo of the game is here: https://notsuho.github.io/ecoland-builds-test-repo/

In this current version, Econews is a minigame component in a larger game Ecoland, designed by Anjalee Wanigarathne in her master's thesis.
Ecoland is a game concept desgined to educate and engage people in climate change action. 

We integrated Econews as part of a broader game storyline on purpose to reach more players and make their overall game experience more varied and enjoyable.
